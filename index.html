Troubleshooting
  • Blank frame or "refused to connect" – your realm blocks framing. A Quickbase realm admin must turn off the security policy Prevent embedding in iframes (realm security policies in the Quickbase Admin Console).
  • Sign-in page appears / keeps asking to sign in – click Sign in to Quickbase…, sign in, close that window. SSO login pages (Okta, Entra ID…) usually refuse to load inside frames, which is why sign-in happens in a separate window.
  • Signed in but frame still shows sign-in (PowerPoint on the web) – the browser is blocking third-party cookies. Allow cookies for [*.]quickbase.com in your browser's site settings (Safari blocks these by default; use Edge or Chrome).
  • Settings lost – settings are stored inside the .pptx. Save the presentation after configuring.
